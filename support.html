Support & Contact

For support with Zemara, please contact Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries using the contact information provided on the Zemara Google Play Store listing or through the official Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries pages.

When requesting support

It is helpful to include a brief description of the problem, the version of Zemara you are using, and your Android device model. Please do not send music files or other personal files unless specifically requested for troubleshooting.

Privacy questions

Questions about Zemara’s handling of data can also be directed to Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries through the same contact information.
